# Mindcraft: Observation Training — outcomes

- [x] Installable, offline-capable PWA
  - Provide an installable PWA with a responsive app shell, offline access to previously loaded training content, and clear update/install states.

- [x] Daily training session and interactive exercises
  - Provide a daily training session composed of short, varied exercises across observation, psychology, criminology, behavioural techniques, and strategic thinking.
  - Include interactive exercise formats such as detail recall, contradiction spotting, evidence evaluation, scenario analysis, sequencing, and strategic decision-making.
  - Give immediate exercise feedback that explains reasoning, distinguishes observation from inference, and highlights alternative interpretations.

- [x] Progressive training program
  - Provide a structured progressive training program with skill tracks, levels, prerequisites, and increasing difficulty over time.
  - Include daily goals and adaptive exercise selection based on recent performance and under-practised skill areas.

- [x] Progress and baseline
  - Provide a progress dashboard showing completion, scores, streaks, skill-area trends, and training history.
  - Include onboarding and baseline assessment to establish goals, explain the practical skill-building approach, and recommend a starting level.
  - Use local device persistence for progress; the approved database power-up was not selected, so do not add accounts or cross-device sync.

- [x] Ethics, consent, and uncertainty
  - Apply ethics and uncertainty guardrails throughout the experience, emphasizing consent, non-manipulation, probabilistic language, bias awareness, and that behavioural cues are not proof.
  - Frame this as practical skill-building rather than literal mind-reading; do not present guesses or behavioral cues as certainty.
  - Daily reminders were not selected; keep reminders optional/absent rather than requesting notification permission.
